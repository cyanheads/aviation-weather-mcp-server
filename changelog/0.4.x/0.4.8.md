# 0.4.8 — 2026-09-22

## Added

- **`aviation_get_metar` bbox area survey** — `bbox` surveys every reporting station in a box, mutually exclusive with `station_ids`; the draw is deduped to the latest observation per station, ordered by station ID, and bounded by the new `limit` (1–400). Truncation and limit disclosures mirror `aviation_find_stations`/`aviation_get_pireps`. [#43](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/43)

## Changed

- **`station_ids`/`station_id` on `aviation_get_metar`, `aviation_get_taf`, `aviation_get_pireps`, and `aviation_preflight_brief`'s ICAO args now accept digit-bearing identifiers** (`/^[A-Z0-9]{4}$/`, e.g. `K0S9`, `KS52`) — 457 of 7,737 current METAR-reporting stations were previously rejected outright. [#37](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/37)
- **`aviation_get_pireps`' `altitude_min_ft`/`altitude_max_ft` bounded to 0–60000 ft**, the range AWC actually searches; the preflight-brief prompt clamps its derived cruise band to match. [#45](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/45)
- **AWC's own rejection text now surfaces as the error message on any 4xx carrying its `{status, error}` envelope**, on every tool, in place of a bare `Fetch failed … Status: 400`. [#45](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/45)

## Fixed

- **`aviation_get_metar` no longer renders a no-wind-group observation as a variable wind** — `not reported` now distinguishes an absent wind group from `VRB`. [#38](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/38)
- **`aviation_get_taf` surfaces text AWC left undecoded** (usually a cloud layer past its three-layer limit) in the new `not_decoded` field, on the period upstream attached it to, instead of dropping it silently. [#39](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/39)
- **`aviation_get_pireps` throws a typed `station_not_recognized` rejection** for a `station_id` AWC's PIREP endpoint doesn't resolve (e.g. a closed airport), with recovery pointing at `aviation_find_stations` and `bbox`. [#45](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/45)

## Dependencies
