# 0.7.2 — 2026-11-13

## Added

- **Historical weather.** Use `as_of` to query METARs, issued TAFs, and PIREPs within AWC’s 30-day retention. METAR and PIREP lookback ceilings are now 720 and 48 hours respectively. Named METAR lookups disclose when the upstream row cap could explain missing stations. ([#66](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/66))
- **Regional surveys.** Query METARs by US state, or TAFs by bounding box or US state. State selectors describe weather available from AWC. ([#67](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/67))
- **Decoded TAF fields.** Read BECMG transition end, forecast altimeter, temperature extrema, icing and turbulence layers, bulletin remarks, and amendment status. ([#68](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/68))
- **Result paging.** Use `offset`, `limit`, and `nextOffset` for METAR and TAF surveys, PIREPs, station area searches, and advisories. Each page uses the currently available draw and retains upstream coverage disclosures. ([#69](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/69))

## Changed

- **Result limits.** Oversized limits on METAR and TAF surveys, PIREPs, and station area searches clamp to 400 with a notice. Set `AWC_CLAMP_LIMITS=false` to reject them. ([#70](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/70))
- **Framework tooling.** Synchronized development skills, verification scripts, and packaging tooling. The minimum Bun version is 1.4.4.

## Fixed

- **Preflight brief.** Scope PIREPs and advisories to route boxes or an airport-based approximation, with coverage gaps disclosed. Use TAF validity, BECMG timing, and active TEMPO and PROB overlays to distinguish prevailing and conditional conditions. ([#71](https://github.com/cyanheads/aviation-weather-mcp-server/issues/71))
